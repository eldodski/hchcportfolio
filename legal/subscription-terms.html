2. Billing and Payment

2.1 Tier 1 (Flat Fee)

Tier 1 services are billed on a per-project basis. The fee is $1,000 per room for a full design consultation and presentation. Additional iterations beyond the initial delivery are billed at $250 per iteration. Tier 1 is not a recurring subscription. Payment is due upon project initiation or as otherwise agreed in writing.

2.2 Tier 2 and Tier 3 (Monthly Subscriptions)

Tier 2 and Tier 3 subscriptions are billed monthly on a recurring basis. Your subscription will automatically renew each month unless you cancel before the next billing date.

Tier 2 Render Credits: Tier 2 subscriptions include 3 render credits annually. Render credits reset on your subscription anniversary date. Unused render credits do not roll over to the next year and carry no cash value.

3. Cancellation and Downgrade Policy

You may cancel or downgrade your subscription at any time through your account settings or by contacting us directly.

Access After Cancellation or Downgrade: When you cancel or downgrade your subscription, you will retain full access to all features through the end of your current billing cycle. No prorated refunds will be issued for partial billing periods.

After your billing cycle ends following a cancellation or downgrade:

  • Download access will be revoked. You will no longer be able to download presentations, renders, or other deliverables from the platform.
  • Records remain visible. Your project history, presentations, and associated data will remain viewable on the platform in a read-only state.
  • You may resubscribe at any time to restore full access, including download capabilities.

4. Refund Policy

Monthly subscription fees are non-refundable once the billing cycle has begun. For Tier 1 flat-fee services, refund eligibility will be determined on a case-by-case basis and must be requested in writing within 14 days of payment.

5. Price Changes

We reserve the right to modify subscription pricing at any time. If we change the price of your subscription, we will provide at least 30 days advance notice before the new price takes effect. Your continued subscription after the price change constitutes acceptance of the new pricing. If you do not agree with the new pricing, you may cancel your subscription before the new rate applies.

6. Payment Methods

We accept payment via the methods made available on the platform. You are responsible for keeping your payment information current and accurate. If a payment fails, we may suspend your access until the outstanding balance is resolved.

7. Free Trial

We may offer free trial periods at our discretion. At the end of a free trial, your subscription will automatically convert to a paid subscription unless you cancel before the trial period ends. We will notify you before any charges are applied.

8. Account Suspension for Non-Payment

If payment for a subscription renewal is not received within 7 days of the billing date, we reserve the right to suspend your account. Suspended accounts will not have access to platform features until payment is received. After 30 days of non-payment, we may terminate the account.
