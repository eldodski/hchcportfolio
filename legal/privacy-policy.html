Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026 | Version 1.0 | DRAFT - Subject to Review

Hill Country Home Concepts ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use our website and platform (the "Service"). By using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account, use our services, or contact us, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, company name, role, and other details provided during registration.
  • Profile Information: Business details, service preferences, and tier selections.
  • Project Data: Design preferences, room specifications, material selections, uploaded images, and presentation content.
  • Communications: Messages, feedback, and correspondence you send to us.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed through our third-party payment provider. We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access the Service, we automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on the platform, and interaction patterns.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, referring URLs, and error logs.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

When you sign in using Google Single Sign-On (SSO) through our authentication provider (Clerk), we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Purpose Data Used

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Service:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication, session management, and security. These cannot be disabled while using the platform.
  • Authentication Cookies: Set by our authentication provider (Clerk) to maintain your signed-in state.
  • Preference Cookies: Store your settings and preferences (such as sidebar state).

We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics tracking cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and notify users.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the platform. Each service has its own privacy policy governing its handling of your data:

  • Clerk (authentication): Manages user accounts, sign-in, and Google SSO. Clerk Privacy Policy
  • Supabase (data storage): Hosts our database and stores user profiles, project data, and platform content. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google (SSO provider): Provides single sign-on authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Vercel (hosting): Hosts the platform website. Vercel Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices:

  • Data is stored in Supabase cloud infrastructure with row-level security (RLS) policies to ensure users can only access their own data.
  • Authentication is handled through Clerk with encrypted sessions and secure token management.
  • All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Administrative access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

While we implement reasonable security measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Service. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal, regulatory, or legitimate business purposes.

Project data and presentation records may be retained in anonymized form for platform improvement purposes after account deletion.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Data Portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by posting the updated policy on the platform and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
