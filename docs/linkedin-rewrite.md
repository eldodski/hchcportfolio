## 2. About / Summary

I run design center operations for production and custom homebuilders in the San Antonio and Texas Hill Country market, managing the full lifecycle from job initiation through installation reconciliation across multiple builder programs simultaneously.

At United Finishes, I oversee the design selection process for builders including Pulte Group, coordinating buyer-facing appointments, vendor relationships, and internal workflows through PHD, RFMS, and M3 systems. I manage a direct report and serve as the operational bridge between sales teams, purchasing, field crews, and homebuyers. Every selection has a budget, a timeline, and a family waiting to move in. My job is making sure all three stay aligned.

What sets me apart is that I have built this capability from the ground up. I understand what it takes to formalize a design center where one did not previously exist: the systems, the staffing model, the vendor strategy, the buyer experience framework. That is not theoretical. I have done it.

Outside of my role at United Finishes, I founded Hill Country Home Concepts, an independent interior design practice serving residential clients and builders across the San Antonio and Hill Country corridor. HCHC is where I apply the same operational rigor to private clients who want design leadership without the overhead of a full-time hire.

I am currently pursuing my RIDQC certification through the Alamo Colleges program to formalize the expertise I have built through years of hands-on work in residential construction.

I am open to design center leadership opportunities, particularly with builders looking to establish or elevate a formalized design program. I am also available for design consultations through HCHC at hillcountryhomeconcepts.com.
